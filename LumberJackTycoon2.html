Lumber Jack Tycoon 2

Overview of the game

Lumber Tycoon 2 is one of the most iconic sandbox games on Roblox, created by Defaultio. It expands on the original Lumber Tycoon with a large open world, deeper progression, and a strong trading and transport economy.

Sawmill Guide

Compare the different sawmills available in Lumber Tycoon 2. Each machine has different costs, log size limits and uses.

Sawmill Types & Specifications
Sawmill Price Max Log Width Max Log Length Description
Shabby Sawmill $130 1.4 units 10.9 units Beginner-friendly. A low-cost starter machine. Smaller logs may be needed to fit within its input limits.
Fair Sawmill $1,600 1.6 units 10.9 units Early progression. An affordable upgrade for processing smaller logs as you expand your operation.
Sawmax 01 $11,000 2.0 units 10.9 units Mid-tier machine. Offers a larger input width and supports output planks up to a 3 × 2 stud cross-section.
Sawmax 02 $22,500 2.6 units 10.9 units Large-log processing. Provides a wider input capacity than the Sawmax 01.
Sawmax 02L $86,500 2.6 units 18.9 units Long-log processing. Combines a wide input capacity with a longer maximum log length.

Tip: Check the dimensions of your logs before feeding them into a sawmill.
